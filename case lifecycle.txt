	case lifecycle

step 1
assess and interview
end of step 1

step 2
select appropriate intervention

notify client with documentary needs

documentary needs must be uploaded by the client remotely and pass on-site
or pass on-site directly

after accomplishing these documents
PCV and COE release 
end of step 2

step 3 
determine the need for inter-agency referrals
yes or no
end of step 3

step 4
evaluate help given
if not self-sufficient -> subject to case renewal
if self-sufficient -> subject to Closure
end of step 4

step 5
evaluate case study
end of step 5

PCV, COE, IRF(optional) and GIS is altogether is a Case Study Report
